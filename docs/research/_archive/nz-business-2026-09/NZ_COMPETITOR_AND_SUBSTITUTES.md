## 3. One-liner

A cited, model-agnostic map of **who already owns NZ attention, trust, and fulfillment** — platforms, directories, media, SaaS, agencies, DIY/community, B2B substitutes, and startup/job-ad *signals* — so invent-or-adapt creative value models can be designed against real substitutes without locking a niche or cloning a unicorn.

---

## 4. Core research question (competitors-level)

Across **product / media / connect / hybrid** (and format-as-mechanic) families, which **direct players and substitutes** already own demand capture or fulfillment in New Zealand — and where do **gaps in distinctive mechanic + tone + distribution** remain open for a solo digital operator — such that Step 4 archetypes can be scored without pretending the field is empty or that one overseas marketplace is the pattern to copy?

### Trust ownership sketch (HYPOTHESIS informed by Steps 0–2)

1. **Platform trust** (Trade Me, SEEK, Builderscrack reviews) substitutes for personal reputation at national scale.
2. **Neighbour / group trust** (Neighbourly, Facebook Groups, WOM) still beats cold SEO for many *local services* decisions — **HYPOTHESIS**.
3. **Institutional trust** (Consumer NZ tests; Canstar stars; licence badges) owns “who to believe” in crowded claim spaces.
4. **Advisor trust** (accountant, lawyer, mortgage broker) owns B2B tool and finance adjacent distribution.
5. **Solo operator trust** must be *earned* via distinctive utility + honest claims (FTA/CGA/Privacy baseline) — aligned with Latent-class originality, not doorway spam.

## 10. Implications for invent-or-adapt (not unicorn clone)

1. **Substitutes are the real competitors (INTENT):** Facebook Groups, Neighbourly recommendations, DIY Google + phone-around, franchise brands, **accountants, agencies, in-house hires, and overseas SaaS** often beat “another marketplace app.” Score against *behaviours*, not only funded startups.
2. **Attention oligopoly ≠ idea oligopoly (HYPOTHESIS):** Stuff/Herald/Trade Me/SEEK/Xero own channels; originality of **mechanic + tone + loop** remains open — Latent-class metaphor, not TV franchise. Mid-layer media (Idealog, Spinoff, creators) shows owned-audience is possible outside mastheads.
3. **Do not clone Urban Company (or any unicorn) (INTENT):** Builderscrack + Hire A Hubby + Trade Me + WOM already cover much of the local-services connect surface. A clone fights incumbents *and* substitutes *and* visa/licence friction.
4. **Connect remains optional (INTENT):** Product, media, agency-lite, comparison-hybrid, B2B pipeline shapes, and invented formats deserve **equal** survey weight in Step 4. Reader must not conclude the program is “NZ delivery marketplace” or “build pipelines for startups.”
5. **Trust theatre is table stakes (EVIDENCE → INTENT):** Reviews, licence badges, Consumer NZ-style independence, and substantiated claims are how NZ buyers filter — design for credibility.
6. **Distribution rent is real (EVIDENCE):** Meta/Google/Trade Me/SEEK tax attention; IAB shows classifieds+search+video wallets. Owned audience (signup) matters *because* rented channels dominate.
7. **White space is plural (HYPOTHESIS):** G1–G11 span capability education, place originality, format, hybrid pipeline, and vertical product — still **hypotheses**, not picks.
8. **PSW + partner options stay OPEN (INTENT):** Until Immigration NZ / licensed adviser verification, down-rank wedges that require employees or thick personal licences (Step 2). Keep **solo and citizen-partnered** structures as **OPEN/verify** — do not green-light employing others.
9. **Job ads and startup lists are research methods (INTENT):** Pain clues and example builders — not demand proof and not a jobs-product thesis.
10. **Next card:** Step 4 turns this map into **model archetypes** with fit/gap rows (incl. institutions + substitutes columns) — still no niche lock.
