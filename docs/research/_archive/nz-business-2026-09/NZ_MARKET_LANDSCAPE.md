## 6. Geography & urban demand map (cited)

*Figures are provisional Stats NZ estimated resident population (ERP) unless noted. Prefer “as of [period]”; revisions expected.*

### National scale

- **EVIDENCE:** At **30 June 2025** (provisional), NZ’s resident population was about **5.3 million**, growing **0.7%** in the year (slower than 1.7% in YE Jun 2024 and 2.3% in YE Jun 2023). ([Stats NZ news — Population growth slows in all regions](https://www.stats.govt.nz/news/population-growth-slows-in-all-regions/); download tables / Infometrics republish **5,324,700**. Accessed 2026-09-11.)
- **EVIDENCE:** **Nearly 2 in 3** people (**64%**) live in **Auckland or one of NZ’s 12 cities**; of city dwellers, just over half (**53%**) live in Auckland; Christchurch ~**12%** of city dwellers; Hamilton and Wellington ~**6%** each. ([Stats NZ — Subnational population estimates: At 30 June 2025](https://www.stats.govt.nz/information-releases/subnational-population-estimates-at-30-june-2025/). Accessed 2026-09-11.)
- **INTENT / implication:** City-scoped and Auckland-weighted research axes are rational for density — **not** a niche or model pick.

### Regional GDP concentration (economic demand density)

- **EVIDENCE:** Year ended **March 2025** (provisional region totals): **Auckland ~37.5%** of national GDP (**~$161.8b**); **Canterbury ~12.9%** (**~$55.5b**); **Wellington ~11.9%** (**~$51.3b**). ([Stats NZ news — Southland leads regional GDP increase…](https://www.stats.govt.nz/news/southland-leads-regional-gdp-increase-in-year-to-march-2025/); Figure.NZ republish of Stats NZ regional GDP. Accessed 2026-09-11.)
- **EVIDENCE (industry colour, YE Mar 2024 balanced):** Auckland’s largest industry GDP slices include **professional / scientific / technical**, **finance & insurance**, and **construction**; Wellington’s include **professional services** and **public administration**. ([Figure.NZ charts from Stats NZ Regional GDP by industry](https://figure.nz/chart/W9VhJ662mcUp87Ny-5C03KEc3fykZvNMJ). Accessed 2026-09-11. Treat industry $ as republished Stats NZ — confirm on Infoshare when unfreezing.)
- **HYPOTHESIS:** Economic activity is even more Auckland-concentrated than population share — digital reach into Auckland (and secondarily Canterbury / Wellington) matters for many model families, not only local-services connect.

### Growth / soft patches (landscape colour, not niche)

- **EVIDENCE:** YE Jun 2025: **Canterbury, Auckland, Waikato** fastest-growing regions; several cities flat or declining (**Wellington, Porirua, Napier, Nelson, Upper Hutt**); **Hamilton** fastest city growth; **Selwyn / Queenstown-Lakes** among fastest TAs. ([Stats NZ subnational release](https://www.stats.govt.nz/information-releases/subnational-population-estimates-at-30-june-2025/). Accessed 2026-09-11.)
- **HYPOTHESIS:** Growing corridors (Canterbury ring, Hamilton, Queenstown-Lakes) may reward place-aware content sooner than shrinking nodes — test later; do not lock.

## 9. SME / services fragmentation notes (cited)

- **EVIDENCE:** At **February 2025** (provisional): **617,330** enterprises; **74%** had **no paid employees**; **2,840** large enterprises (100+) held about **50%** of employees in the series; paid employees in these enterprises ~**2.4 million** (−2.2% YoY — not an official employment statistic). Auckland ~**35%** of business locations and employees. ([Stats NZ — BDS At February 2025](https://www.stats.govt.nz/information-releases/new-zealand-business-demography-statistics-at-february-2025/). Accessed 2026-09-11.)
- **EVIDENCE:** MBIE: about **97%** of businesses are small (**&lt;20 FTEs**). ([MBIE — Strong foundations…](https://www.mbie.govt.nz/business-and-employment/economic-growth/going-for-growth/going-for-growth-february-2025-report/strong-foundations-to-foster-economic-growth). Accessed 2026-09-11.)
- **EVIDENCE:** Size-band republish: **455,730** zero-employee enterprises vs ~**161.6k** with employees (Figure.NZ from Stats NZ BDS). ([Figure.NZ all industries size](https://figure.nz/chart/8vpXvYloRqFDQXIE-e76uY4ho6rmWjidL). Accessed 2026-09-11.)
- **HYPOTHESIS:** Extreme SME fragmentation + services-heavy GDP supports **many** model families (content, productised expertise, media, light connect, hybrid). It does **not** prove marketplace unit economics at NZ scale.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** Trust friction (Consumer Survey) + Fair Trading norms (charter §10) favour **useful, accurate** digital assets over spam doorways — aligned with Latent-class originality (distinctive mechanic/audience/distribution), not mass thin pages.

### 9.1 B2B / SME demand colour & hiring texture (widen; not niche)

*Qualitative demand colour for later cards. No TAM. Not a jobs-product or “pipeline” lock — see Step 3 job-ad-as-signal method.*

- **EVIDENCE (MBIE business health, Jul 2024):** Among surveyed businesses: **58%** report ongoing challenge (cashflow / growth slowdown); **36%** face profitability squeeze (rising costs + weaker customer acquisition); **28%** focusing on growth; only **18%** list “become more digital” as a priority; top support asks include tax breaks (**47%**), one-to-one mentoring (**32%**), online courses (**29%**); barriers cluster on **cost, time, uncertainty**. ([MBIE — Business health and motivations research](https://www.mbie.govt.nz/business-and-employment/business/support-for-business/research-and-reports-for-small-business/business-health-and-motivations-research); [motivations PDF](https://www.mbie.govt.nz/dmsdocument/28986-business-health-and-digital-monitor-july-2024). Accessed 2026-09-13.)
- **EVIDENCE (recap + texture):** Digital-capability monitor: ~**40%** use digital marketing/SEO/ads; support demand clusters on **choosing tools** and **digital marketing**; security / time / tool-choice remain barriers (§8). Combined with health monitor: many firms feel pressure **before** they prioritise “more digital.”
- **EVIDENCE (filled jobs, industry):** March **2025** quarter: **2.26 million** filled jobs; soft patches included construction (−1.4% q/q seasonally adjusted), professional/scientific/technical (−0.6%), health care & social assistance (−0.3%); accommodation & food +0.5%. ([Stats NZ — Business employment data: March 2025 quarter](https://www.stats.govt.nz/information-releases/business-employment-data-march-2025-quarter/). Accessed 2026-09-13.)
- **EVIDENCE (occupation texture, Census 2023 republish):** **Advertising and marketing professionals** (ANZSCO 2251) are **Auckland-heavy** (Auckland ~**10.2k** of a national count on the order of **~19k** when summing regional Figure.NZ cells — treat as republished Census; quality caveats on Figure.NZ page). ([Figure.NZ ← Stats NZ Census 2023](https://figure.nz/chart/fuCYWwu9zhYA6UCf). Accessed 2026-09-13.) Fine-grain “digital marketing hire rate” = **UNKNOWN** at Tier A.
- **INTENT:** B2B/SME colour (cost/time pressure + digital under-prioritisation + urban marketing-occupation density) is a **landscape texture** for product / media / hybrid / agency-lite surveys — **not** a niche pick and **not** clearance to hire (Step 2 PSW = **OPEN/verify**).
- **HYPOTHESIS:** Firms that *do* hire for sales, ops, or marketing are revealing paid pain; job ads are a **signal method** for Step 3 — not proof of demand for a solo operator product.

## 10. Implications for ecosystem research (not niche pick; invent-or-adapt open)

1. **Density first, model second (INTENT):** Research and early tests should respect **Auckland + major cities** demand concentration and Auckland’s outsized GDP share — while leaving **regional / tourism / growth-corridor** pockets open for distinctive mechanics.
2. **Services economy = plural models (HYPOTHESIS):** Business services, arts/recreation, retail/accommodation, health, construction, and tourism-adjacent demand each suggest different **mechanic × distribution** shapes (format, product, media, connect, hybrid). Survey many; lock none here.
3. **Levers stay model-agnostic (INTENT):** SEO, ads, signup, product, and connect remain **levers**. Marketplace-style connect is **one option among many**, not the north star. Do not set Urban Company (or any unicorn) as mandate.
4. **Latent bound (INTENT):** Distinctive creative value = originality of **mechanic / audience / distribution** that fits NZ. **Not** an entertainment/TV production plan.
5. **SME gap as opportunity space (HYPOTHESIS):** Many firms lack active SEO/digital marketing (MBIE ~40%) — fertile for tools, education products, agency-lite, and owned-audience formats — **or** invented hybrids — without requiring a two-sided platform.
6. **Anti-spam / trust (EVIDENCE → INTENT):** High online purchase rates coexist with security concern and low habitual trust of sales claims — design later wedges for **credibility and utility**.
7. **Falsify geography myths early (INTENT):** Do not assume national TAM from Auckland density alone; do not assume small cities have zero opportunity for niche originality — score in Steps 4–5.
8. **B2B colour without niche lock (INTENT):** Cost/time pressure + digital under-prioritisation (MBIE) and hiring texture are landscape signals for plural families — not a “pipelines for startups” thesis (→ Step 3 widen / [`NZ_COMPILE_0_3.md`](./NZ_COMPILE_0_3.md)).
9. **Next cards:** Institutions/visa filter (Step 2) → competitors/substitutes across model families (Step 3) → invent-or-adapt archetypes (Step 4) → capped wedges (Step 5).
