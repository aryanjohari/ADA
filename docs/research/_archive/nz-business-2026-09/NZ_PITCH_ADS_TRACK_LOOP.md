## Slide 1 — One-liner

**I run a capped Google campaign in your name, in one belt. You get the phone numbers. I message you daily until we know if it quoted and if it made money. You see spend vs cash. You own the accounts if you leave.**

Not: “be more digital.” Not: I answer your customers as receptionist. Not: we rank 80 suburb pages.

---

## Slide 2 — Who / constraint

| | |
|--|--|
| **Operator** | Solo digital (SEO/ads/pages). Indian citizen, NZ **post-study work** — employing others = **OPEN / verify** (often a blocker). |
| **Money goal (INTENT)** | ~**NZD $15–20k/mo** from ~**15–40** paying accounts — not 800 consumer subs. |

---

## Slide 3 — The problem (cited colour, not a TAM)

NZ SMEs feel **costs + weaker customer acquisition**. Only **~18%** list “become more digital” as a priority. **~40%** already use SEO/ads (MBIE). Felt tech ROI is weak (CPA **Tier B**). Groups / DIY Google / nephew sit at **$0**.

They will not pay for “marketing education.” They might pay if **enquiries show up and don’t die**, and if **spend vs money** is visible.

---

## Slide 4 — Offer (SKU)

**Start lock (change industry if 5 conversations fail — not forever):** one **B2B/commercial** niche in **one employment belt** (e.g. Penrose–East Tāmaki–Airport-class). Default colour from research: facilities / commercial cleaning-type firms — **operator does not do the trade**.

### They pay

- **Setup (once):** one landing page + Google Business Profile tidy + Google Ads account + call/form tracking. Small one-off or folded into month 1.
- **Retainer:** **$495–$750/mo** to the operator (inside published NZ single-channel band; Jelly-class ads management from **$495**).
- **Ad spend:** **theirs**, capped (e.g. $30–$80/day), paid **to Google**, visible on the receipt. Not buried in the $495.

### Operator does

- One Search/PMax campaign: **their** service, **that** belt.
- Same-day **pass-through of numbers** (not middleman on the call).
- **Daily message** to the SME: quoted Y/N? amount? won $? (Twilio/WhatsApp **to the client**; ADA-style log later).
- Weekly **receipt:** Google $X → N enquiries → N quotes → $ won.

### They keep on cancel

Ads account, GBP, page, lead history. Operator stops logging in. No hostage logins.

### Not in the offer

Rank promises, Meta/TikTok/brand/full site rebuild, “20 jobs,” operator paying media from the retainer, **5 free leads**, pay-per-lead as the door, AI phone bot (Later), national pSEO.

---

## Slide 5 — Why this vs a $995 agency

Same **thin channel** SKU the market already priced. Difference is the **close-the-loop receipt** (quote → money), not more dashboards.

Google already automates **bidding** inside a cap. What’s left: **setup, publish/approve claims, daily nag, reading the receipt.** That is the product. Full chase-as-receptionist **does not scale to 15** (PSW; hours). Graduation:

| Stage | Who talks to the lead | Why they pay |
|-------|------------------------|--------------|
| First ~1–3 clients | Operator may chase more (learn) | Proof |
| Scale | **SME** calls; operator **nags + ads + log** | Leads don’t die; spend vs cash |
| Or stay small | Operator stays receptionist | **4–8** seats at **$1.5–3k**, not 15 × $500 |

---

## Slide 6 — 2–3 week test (not a ranking project)

1. One offer page (operator) + 2–4 useful receipts for **that** job — not a how-to encyclopedia.
2. Small ads **to that offer** (get the first SME) **or** five belt conversations / door-walk.
3. First paying (or dated LOI) firm: **their** ads, **their** page.
4. Count: **paid retainer or completed 14-day loop**, not GSC clicks.
5. Kill if: clicks and no yes; or they won’t answer the daily ping.

Operator’s own site is **only** how SMEs find *this* offer. It is **not** the client’s lead engine and **not** a Moneymaker pSEO grid “while SEO cooks.”

**~$1k test budget:** spend on **one** loop, not 2–3 parallel businesses.

---

## Slide 7 — Market (don’t confuse layers)

- **IAB CY 2025:** NZ digital ads **~$2.97b**; Search **~$1.44b**. That rent is **Google/Meta/classifieds**, not an agency’s “share.”
- **SME management fees:** fragmented (Jelly-class **$495–$1,500** one channel). Easy to enter, hard to own.
- **~60%** of firms don’t run SEO/ads ≠ they will pay. Gap ≠ WTP.

## Open questions (for the two entrepreneur AIs)

1. Will a belt SME pay **$495–$750 + spend** for number-pass + daily close-the-loop, vs DIY Google or Groups?
2. Is **commercial cleaning / facilities** the wrong first vertical — what lighter-compliance B2B would you test instead (still one belt)?
3. At 15 accounts, is the nag-not-receptionist retainer **real**, or must this stay **4–8 fat seats**?
4. What would make you **kill** this in 14 days besides “no calls”?
5. What is the **smallest** setup that still looks trustworthy (FTA / who you are) without a page factory?

## One-paragraph recap (copy-paste)

Working model: solo NZ operator sells **one-channel Google Ads + landing + GBP + same-day number pass + daily quote/won tracking** to **one commercial niche in one employment belt**, fee **~$495–$750/mo + client ad spend**, client owns accounts. Goal band **$15–20k from 15–40 accounts** only works if hours-per-client fall (they call; operator nags) or if fewer clients pay more. Research pack: sell customers/cost/time not “digital”; one live test; no doorway pSEO; PSW hiring unverified. Adjacent SKUs (desk, tender pack, honest pages, affiliate) stay on the shelf until this loop has a paid yes or a kill.

## Prompts (copy-paste)

### Prompt A — inference / entrepreneur critique

Use **twice**, on **two different AIs**. Attach or paste the **full** pitch (`NZ_PITCH_ADS_TRACK_LOOP.md`). Do not add extra ideas in the first message.

```text
You are a sceptical entrepreneur and operator, not a cheerleader. You know small-business sales, Google Ads, and New Zealand SME reality well enough to attack weak offers. You are not an immigration lawyer.

Read the attached pitch in full (slides + adjacent table + operator idea inventory + open questions).

Your job is INFERENCE: what is this company actually, what has to be true for it to work, and where it dies.

Rules:
- Do not invent TAM, CPC, or WTP dollars. Mark unknowns as unknown.
- Do not recommend doorway pSEO, scraped lead lists, three live businesses in week one, or “just automate it.”
- Do not treat IAB NZ digital ad spend as this operator’s addressable market.
- Visa/hiring: flag friction; do not invent permissions.
- Fair Trading Act: punish promised lead counts and fake scarcity.
- Take the idea inventory seriously: do not reopen branches already marked drop unless you have a sharp reason.

Write in this structure (short, sharp):

1. What this really is (3–5 sentences)
2. What must be true (bullet assumptions)
3. Where it dies (substitutes, scale/receptionist, price, vertical, solo/PSW)
4. Strongest version of the offer (one paragraph — may tighten SKU, price, or vertical)
5. Answers to the pitch’s open questions (1–5)
6. Verdict — exactly three lines:
   KEEP: …
   KILL: …
   CHANGE: …

No motivational padding. No new 12-week roadmaps. No building an OS.
```
