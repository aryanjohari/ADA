## 3. One-liner

A cited, model-agnostic survey of **how value can loop** in New Zealand (demand capture ↔ owned audience ↔ fulfillment/connect ↔ learning) and of **invent-or-adapt archetypes** across format, product, media, connect, and hybrid — so Step 5 can score wedges without cloning a unicorn or locking a niche.

---

## 4. Core research question (ecosystem/models-level)

Which **creative value-model archetypes** (distinctive mechanic + tone + distribution / fulfillment loop) are structurally compatible with New Zealand’s **demand density, substitute map, and institutions** — for a **solo digital operator** who can use SEO/ads, owned audience, digital products, and connect as **levers** — such that lightweight loops can prove demand and learning **before** heavy software, and what would **falsify** treating any family (or any overseas unicorn shape) as the default?

## 6. Ecosystem loop (model-agnostic levers)

*Design lock from charter §11. Marketplace-style connect is **optional**. Labels mark what is fact vs framing.*

```text
[ Demand capture ]  →  [ Owned audience ]  →  [ Fulfillment / product / connect ]
   SEO / ads /              signup / list /              kit, course, service,
   useful content           newsletter / account         manual intro, licensed
        ↑                            ↓                   third party, hybrid
        └──────── [ Learning loop ] ←┘
                    GSC / ads / signup / refund-quality
                    (research informs topics; no pipeline code here)
```

### 6.1 Lever definitions (INTENT)

| Lever | What it does | What it is **not** |
|-------|----------------|--------------------|
| **Demand capture** | Brings strangers to a useful surface (search, ads, borrowed social, advisor channel) | A moat by itself; IAB spend shows rented attention is large and incumbent-owned |
| **Owned audience** | Consenting list / relationship the operator can return to | Harvested directories; Meta/Google followings rented from platforms |
| **Fulfillment / product / connect** | Completes the promise (digital good, education, manual intro, licensed-party match, hybrid) | Automatic two-sided marketplace; “we do the restricted trade” |
| **Learning loop** | Measures which topics, claims, and offers convert without lying | ISR/GSC **implementation**; invented CPC/TAM |

### 6.2 NZ-shaped constraints on the loop (cited)

- **EVIDENCE (density):** Demand and enterprises concentrate in cities; Auckland region ~**1.82M** of ~**5.32M** (30 Jun 2025 provisional); ~**64%** live in Auckland or NZ’s 12 cities; Auckland ~**37.5%** of GDP (YE Mar 2025). ([Stats NZ subnational ERP](https://www.stats.govt.nz/information-releases/subnational-population-estimates-at-30-june-2025/); [Stats NZ regional GDP news](https://www.stats.govt.nz/news/southland-leads-regional-gdp-increase-in-year-to-march-2025/). Accessed 2026-09-11 / recap.) **Implication (INTENT):** City-scoped capture is rational; national two-sided liquidity is harder to justify at this population scale (**HYPOTHESIS**).
- **EVIDENCE (SME fragmentation):** **617,330** enterprises at Feb 2025; **74%** zero paid employees; ~**97%** of businesses &lt;20 FTEs (MBIE). ([Stats NZ BDS](https://www.stats.govt.nz/information-releases/new-zealand-business-demography-statistics-at-february-2025/); [MBIE Going for Growth](https://www.mbie.govt.nz/business-and-employment/economic-growth/going-for-growth/going-for-growth-february-2025-report/strong-foundations-to-foster-economic-growth). Accessed 2026-09-11.) **Implication (HYPOTHESIS):** Many counterparties cannot staff a sophisticated buy; lightweight products/media can fit — **or** fail because time/cost barriers stay high (MBIE health monitor: cost, time, uncertainty).
- **EVIDENCE (digital gap, not niche):** Only ~**40%** of businesses promote themselves with digital marketing, SEO, or online ads; support demand clusters on **choosing tools** and **digital marketing**. ([MBIE digital capability monitor, Jul 2024](https://www.mbie.govt.nz/dmsdocument/28920-business-digital-capability-monitor-key-findings-and-update-july-2024-pdf). Accessed 2026-09-11.)
- **EVIDENCE (trust):** **93%** online purchase in last 6 months; **63%** concerned about online shopping security; only **42%** trust salesperson information always/most of the time. ([NZ Consumer Survey 2024](https://www.comcom.govt.nz/assets/pdf_file/0023/361274/Commerce-Commission-and-Ministry-of-Business2C-Innovation-and-Employment-NZ-Consumer-Survey-2024-July-2024.pdf). Accessed 2026-09-11.) Combined with FTA unsubstantiated-claims rules (Step 2) → **INTENT:** honesty is a **loop design constraint**, not a slogan.
- **EVIDENCE (rented attention):** CY 2025 digital ad revenue **$2.967b** (+12% YoY); Search **$1.44b**; Video **$653.8m** (+27%); Classifieds & Directories **$389.5m**; Recruitment **24.4%** and Real Estate **23.2%** of advertiser-category share. ([IAB NZ Q4 CY 2025 report](https://www.iab.org.nz/news-resources/q4-cy-2025-iab-new-zealand-digital-advertising-revenue-report). Accessed 2026-09-13.) **Implication (INTENT):** Ads are a lever that must clear against incumbents’ wallets; owned audience exists *because* this rent is real. Vertical CPC = **UNKNOWN** at Tier A.
- **EVIDENCE (owned-audience law):** Unsolicited Electronic Messages Act 2007: commercial electronic messages with a NZ link need **consent**; accurate **sender identity**; **functional unsubscribe**; **no address-harvesting** software / harvested lists for unsolicited commercial messages. ([DIA — Commercial electronic messaging](https://www.dia.govt.nz/Spam---Commercial-electronic-messaging-in-New-Zealand); [legislation.govt.nz — UEM Act](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2007/0007/latest/dlm405134.html). Accessed 2026-09-13.) Privacy Act still applies to collection (Step 2). **INTENT:** This program’s scraper ban and the UEM harvesting ban **rhyme**; harvested “lead lists” are out as both method and law-shaped filter.
- **UNKNOWN:** Category-level search vs Facebook Groups vs Trade Me vs accountant as *first* touch. Do not invent; measure in Step 5.

### 6.3 Lightweight vs heavy (HYPOTHESIS, for scoring — not a pick)

| Loop weight | Typical shape | NZ-scale comment |
|-------------|----------------|------------------|
| **Light** | Useful pages + consenting list + digital kit or manual intro | Matches solo time; deferred software (charter §11.4); testable via signup/refund |
| **Medium** | Recurring format + paid product + occasional connect | Needs distinctive mechanic so it is not “another agency newsletter” |
| **Heavy** | Two-sided marketplace, hiring network, regulated advice platform | Collides with Step 3 incumbents **and** Step 2 licence/hiring/PSW filters |

**INTENT:** Prefer proving the loop **light** first. Heavy software is a *later* consequence of a surviving wedge, not an opening move.

## 7. Model archetype cards (format / product / media / connect / hybrid + invent variants)

### 7.0 How to read a card

- **IDs** are survey handles for Step 5, **not** ranked picks.
- **Kind:** **adapt** = known family, NZ-fit localisation of *shape* (not of a named unicorn). **Invent** = distinctive mechanic / audience / distribution that is not “the overseas version, but smaller.”
- **Latent test (INTENT):** If the card could be described as “category + ads” with no named ritual, named audience identity, or named distribution twist, it is **under-invented**.
- **PSW row** is always **OPEN / verify** — never auto-green.
- **Tier C** overseas colour = inspiration only.

### 7.1 Format family (mechanic / ritual — not TV)

*Format can sit **inside** any other family. These cards isolate the **ritual** as the value.*

#### A-F-1 · Receipt Ritual · **invent**

| Row | Content |
|-----|---------|
| **Mechanic** | Every public artefact ends with a **receipt**: sources, date, what is still UNKNOWN, what would be corrected. Honesty is the *show*, not a footer. |
| **Audience metaphor** | People who already distrust sales claims (Consumer Survey) and want citable utility. |
| **Distribution metaphor** | Search-indexed receipts + consenting email; not a studio audience. |
| **Demand capture** | SEO/content on questions incumbents answer with puffery; light ads only if claims stay substantiated. |
| **Owned audience** | Signup to “new receipts / corrections,” not a promo blast. UEM consent + unsubscribe + Privacy statement. |
| **Fulfillment / connect** | The receipt *is* the product at first; later can attach a kit (A-P-*) or shortlist (A-C-2). |
| **Learning loop** | Which receipts earn return visits / signups / corrections requested; GSC queries that match “should I trust X” intent. |
| **NZ fit** | **HYPOTHESIS:** High FTA awareness + low habitual trust of sales copy (Steps 0–2) makes a credibility ritual locally distinctive. Urban density supplies enough searchers; does not require national TV reach. |
| **Substitute pressure (Step 3)** | Consumer NZ (tests/membership), Canstar stars, masthead explainers, Facebook “ask the group.” Risk: looks like a weaker Consumer NZ. Differentiate by **named ritual + operator voice**, not by pretending to be a testing lab. |
| **Institutions (Step 2 + UEM)** | Baseline FTA (substantiation), CGA if paid, Privacy + **UEM Act** if emailing. Easy to *claim* receipts and still mislead — FTA still bites. |
| **PSW** | **OPEN / verify.** Solo content/product shape; no employees required. |
| **Falsify this card if…** | Readers treat “receipts” as gimmick; corrections never happen; or the format becomes a broadcast roast show (north-star fail). |

| **Demand capture** | City-scoped pages; occasional local ads; borrowed community posts that *point* to the serial (not spam the group). |
| **Owned audience** | Place-list with UEM consent; identity is “people of X corridor,” not a national blast. |
| **Fulfillment / connect** | Serial first; optional seasonal kit (A-P-3) or curated local shortlist (A-C-2) later. |
| **Learning loop** | Signup rate by place; returning openers; which episodes get search impressions. |
| **NZ fit** | **EVIDENCE** of corridor growth (Canterbury, Hamilton, Queenstown-Lakes — Step 1) + **HYPOTHESIS** that national platforms flatten place (G5). Small enough for a solo host; too small for a second Stuff. |
| **Substitute pressure** | Local Facebook Groups, Neighbourly, Stuff/Herald local, council/tourism sites. **HYPOTHESIS:** groups own *conversation*; serial can own *citable structure* (G2). |

| **PSW** | **OPEN / verify.** Solo media. |
