## 3. One-liner

A trackable, cited research spine for a solo NZ operator to **invent or adapt a distinctive creative value model** that fits New Zealand — surveying many model families (format, product, media, connect, hybrid) — using SEO, ads, owned audience, digital products, and connect as **levers**, without locking a niche or copying any single overseas unicorn.

## 4. Core research question

In New Zealand, which **creative value models** (distinctive mechanic + tone + distribution / fulfillment loop) are viable for a **solo digital operator** with SEO/content/programmatic-page strength — such that early wedges can prove demand and learning **before** heavy build — and what evidence would **falsify** each candidate?

## 6. Why this program (INTENT + thin EVIDENCE)

### Solo digital operator in NZ

- **INTENT:** Build durable digital assets (audience, ranked pages, products, connect workflows) rather than only trading time for fees.
- **EVIDENCE (thin):** NZ demand and business activity concentrate in cities and regions with measurable populations and enterprise counts (see §10). That makes **local SEO / city-scoped pages** a rational research axis — not yet a niche pick.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** A solo operator can reach early proof via search + ads + signup before needing a large sales network.

### Invent / adapt vs clone

- **INTENT:** Prefer a **distinctive** NZ-fit model (mechanic + tone + distribution) over localizing one overseas unicorn.
- **EVIDENCE (thin):** NZ is a **services-heavy** economy with a **very large share of small / zero-employee enterprises** (§10) — fertile ground for many model families (content, product, media, agency, connect, hybrid), not one mandated shape.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** Originality of mechanic/audience/distribution can matter as much as category choice; Steps 3–5 test candidates, including invented ones.

### Ecosystem vs single tactic

- **INTENT:** Treat SEO/ads, owned audience, product/fulfillment/connect, and learning loops as **one system** of levers — **model-agnostic**. Marketplace-style connect is optional, not default.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** Early wedges can validate unit economics with light tools (pages, list, product, manual broker) before software complexity.

### Build-in-public / value / anti-spam

- **INTENT:** Prefer transparent learning and useful pages over doorway spam; reputation compounds in a small market.
- **EVIDENCE (thin):** NZ consumer norms and law emphasise accurate trader information; survey data shows limited habitual trust of sales claims and high concern about online shopping security (§10) — spam and misleading pages are structurally misaligned with both law and trust.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** Useful, citable, locally grounded content outperforms thin doorway clusters over a multi-year horizon.

## 9. Source policy

### Tier A (prefer for hard numbers)

Official NZ statistical and government sources, e.g.:

- [Stats NZ](https://www.stats.govt.nz/) (`stats.govt.nz`)
- [MBIE](https://www.mbie.govt.nz/) (`mbie.govt.nz`)
- [Companies Office / Companies Register](https://companies-register.companiesoffice.govt.nz/) info pages
- [Consumer Protection](https://www.consumerprotection.govt.nz/) · [Commerce Commission](https://www.comcom.govt.nz/) (high-level)
- [Immigration NZ](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/) — **high-level only**; always flag **“verify for your visa / status”** (not counsel)
- [digital.govt.nz](https://www.digital.govt.nz/) or peer official digital-strategy pages when relevant

### Tier B (secondary)

Reputable NZ media / industry summaries (**RNZ, Stuff, NZ Herald, Interest.co.nz**, university/industry surveys such as WIP-NZ / InternetNZ). **Never** sole source for hard numbers that Tier A covers.

### Tier C (context only)

Blogs, vendor whitepapers, overseas creative/business analogies (e.g. format writeups), aggregator “digital reports.” Mark clearly; do not treat as NZ official stats. Overseas examples are **inspiration for originality**, not NZ evidence.

### How to cite

Every non-obvious factual claim: **URL + organisation + access date** in §16. Prefer ranges and “as of [period]” from the source. If unsure → **UNKNOWN** or qualitative — **no invented TAM / GDP to false decimals**.

### Refresh rule

When **unfreezing** a later card (or refreshing Step 0 context): re-check citation dates and Stats NZ/MBIE release versions. Population and business demography figures are often **provisional** and **revised**. Note revision status in the card changelog.

### Method bans (this program)

No scrapers for this research track; no login-walled bulk harvest of business directories; no presenting legal/immigration content as personalised advice.

## 10. Grounded NZ context (cited, SHORT — not full landscape)

*Labels: EVIDENCE = cited · INTENT/HYPOTHESIS = operator framing.*

### Population / urban concentration

- **EVIDENCE:** At **30 June 2025** (provisional), New Zealand’s estimated resident population was about **5.32 million**; the **Auckland region** was about **1.82 million**. ([Stats NZ — subnational population estimates: At 30 June 2025](https://www.stats.govt.nz/information-releases/subnational-population-estimates-at-30-june-2025/); download tables show Auckland **1,816,000** and NZ **5,324,700** — see also [Stats NZ news: Population growth slows in all regions](https://www.stats.govt.nz/news/population-growth-slows-in-all-regions/). Accessed 2026-09-11.)
- **EVIDENCE:** **Nearly 2 in 3** people (about **64%**) live in Auckland or one of New Zealand’s **12 cities**; among city dwellers, just over half (**53%**) live in Auckland. ([Stats NZ — same subnational release](https://www.stats.govt.nz/information-releases/subnational-population-estimates-at-30-june-2025/). Accessed 2026-09-11.)
- **EVIDENCE (national update):** At **30 June 2026** (provisional), national ERP was about **5.36 million** (**5,357,300**). Subnational Auckland breakdown for mid-2026 was not used in this spine; city-share framing above remains from the June 2025 subnational release. ([Stats NZ national population estimates via press summary](https://www.scoop.co.nz/stories/AK2608/S00529/national-population-estimates-at-30-june-2026.htm); confirm on [stats.govt.nz](https://www.stats.govt.nz/) when citing in later cards. Accessed 2026-09-11.)
- **INTENT / implication for later cards:** Local SEO and city-scoped content maps are first-class research axes because demand density is urban-concentrated — **not** because a niche or model family is chosen here.

### Economy sketch

- **EVIDENCE:** In the **September 2025 quarter**, industry-group shares of the economy were approximately **services 74.4%**, **goods-producing 20.4%**, **primary 5.2%**. ([Stats NZ — Gross domestic product: September 2025 quarter](https://www.stats.govt.nz/information-releases/gross-domestic-product-september-2025-quarter/). Accessed 2026-09-11. Note: later quarters report growth; this spine keeps the last clear published industry-group **share** table.)
- **EVIDENCE (qualitative, MBIE):** MBIE describes NZ as predominantly **service-based** (~three-quarters), with goods-producing and primary making up the rest; main export themes include dairy/meat, forestry, and tourism. ([MBIE — Strong foundations to foster economic growth, Feb 2025 Going for Growth material](https://www.mbie.govt.nz/business-and-employment/economic-growth/going-for-growth/going-for-growth-february-2025-report/strong-foundations-to-foster-economic-growth). Accessed 2026-09-11.)
- **UNKNOWN here:** Sector-by-sector TAM for any model family (media, product, connect, etc.) — deferred to Step 1 / 5. Do not invent.

### Digital / search behaviour notes

- **EVIDENCE:** **2023 Census:** about **90.5%** of households had access to the internet (up from **86.1%** in 2018); access is generally higher in urbanised areas and lower in some rural districts. ([Stats NZ — Housing in Aotearoa New Zealand infographic](https://www.stats.govt.nz/infographics/housing-in-aotearoa-new-zealand/); [Stats NZ news — Home ownership increases…](https://www.stats.govt.nz/news/home-ownership-increases-and-housing-quality-improves/). Accessed 2026-09-11.)
- **EVIDENCE:** In the **NZ Consumer Survey 2024** (Commerce Commission & MBIE), **93%** of consumers had made an **online purchase in the last 6 months**; nearly **2 in 3 (63%)** agreed they are concerned about security/safety when shopping online. ([NZ Consumer Survey 2024 PDF](https://www.comcom.govt.nz/assets/pdf_file/0023/361274/Commerce-Commission-and-Ministry-of-Business2C-Innovation-and-Employment-NZ-Consumer-Survey-2024-July-2024.pdf). Accessed 2026-09-11.)
- **UNKNOWN:** NZ Google Search market share, average CPC by vertical, and “programmatic SEO TAM” — not established from Tier A in Step 0. Treat search economics as **OPEN** for Steps 1/3/5.
- **INTENT:** Research may later **inform page topics** and learning loops (GSC/ads/signups); this charter does **not** design ISR/GSC code.

### Small business prevalence

- **EVIDENCE:** At **February 2025** (provisional), NZ had **617,330** enterprises; **74%** had **no paid employees**. Enterprises with **100+** employees numbered **2,840** and accounted for about **50%** of employees in the series. ([Stats NZ — New Zealand business demography statistics: At February 2025](https://www.stats.govt.nz/information-releases/new-zealand-business-demography-statistics-at-february-2025/). Accessed 2026-09-11.)
- **EVIDENCE:** MBIE states about **97%** of businesses are small (**fewer than 20 FTEs**). ([MBIE — Strong foundations…](https://www.mbie.govt.nz/business-and-employment/economic-growth/going-for-growth/going-for-growth-february-2025-report/strong-foundations-to-foster-economic-growth). Accessed 2026-09-11.)
- **HYPOTHESIS:** Fragmented small-enterprise supply and services-heavy demand support **many** entry shapes (content, product, agency, connect, hybrid) — test in Steps 3–5; do not treat as proof of a specific niche or marketplace thesis.

### Trust / consumer norms

- **EVIDENCE:** The **Fair Trading Act** prohibits misleading/deceptive conduct in trade (incl. online advertising); Consumer Protection and the Commerce Commission publish plain-language rights material. ([Consumer Protection — Fair Trading Act](https://www.consumerprotection.govt.nz/general-help/consumer-laws/fair-trading-act); [Commerce Commission — Your rights as a consumer](https://www.comcom.govt.nz/consumers/your-rights-as-a-consumer/). Accessed 2026-09-11.)
- **EVIDENCE:** NZ Consumer Survey 2024: only **42%** of consumers trust that information from salespeople is fair and accurate “always” or “most of the time” (down from 45% in 2022); awareness that consumer laws exist is high (**94%**), with high awareness of CGA/FTA (**~89–90%**). ([NZ Consumer Survey 2024](https://www.comcom.govt.nz/assets/pdf_file/0023/361274/Commerce-Commission-and-Ministry-of-Business2C-Innovation-and-Employment-NZ-Consumer-Survey-2024-July-2024.pdf). Accessed 2026-09-11.)
- **INTENT:** Anti-spam / honest claims are design locks for later page and outreach work — aligned with both law and measured trust friction.

## 11. Ecosystem principles (design lock for later cards)

1. **Loop, not tactic:** Demand capture (SEO / ads / content) ↔ **owned audience** (signup / newsletter) ↔ **fulfillment / product / connect** ↔ **learning loop** (GSC / ads / signup metrics). Research informs topics; implementation of page pipelines is later.
2. **Model-agnostic levers:** SEO, ads, signup, product, and connect are **levers** available to many model families — not a marketplace-first mandate.
3. **Invent or adapt:** Prefer distinctive mechanic + tone + distribution that fits NZ; survey many models; leave room to invent. Do not lock a single overseas unicorn as the pattern to localize.
4. **Heavy software deferred:** Manual broker / agency / lightweight product allowed as entry; full marketplace (or other heavy platform) deferred until wedges justify complexity.
5. **Parallel niches = scorecards first;** live builds **capped** (Step 5 owns caps).
6. **Value > doorways:** No spam doorway endorsement; build-in-public and real utility preferred.
7. **Separate cash track:** Near-term cash may exist outside this research program; do not confuse day-rate survival with asset thesis success.
8. **Latent bound:** Creative-model metaphor only — not an entertainment/TV production plan.
