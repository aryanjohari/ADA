A cited, plural survey of **business-model families** that could plausibly deliver **NZD $15,000–$20,000** monthly recurring revenue (or equivalent stable monthly cash that can be productised into MRR) for a **solo / very small** NZ operator — cash arithmetic first, niche lock deferred.

### 4.1 Targets

| Target | Monthly | Annualised (×12) | Notes |

GST: IR threshold to register is **$60k in 12 months** (Step 2). At $15–20k/mo you are **well above** — accountant verify. Figures below are **ex-GST unless a source says otherwise**.

### 4.2 What “15–20 people” can and cannot mean

| Reading | Fits $15–20k? | Comment |
|---------|---------------|---------|
| **15–40 B2B accounts** each paying $500–$1,333/mo | **Yes — design target** | Agency, lead desk, commercial packs, vertical SaaS seats, retainers |
| **15–40 advertisers** sponsoring a niche media property | **Maybe** | Needs high CPM / sponsorship tickets (Idealog-class rates exist — §6); audience still large |
| **15–40 consumers** on $19/mo membership | **No** | 15 × $19 = $285; needs **~790–1,050** subs at $19 (The Kākā regular price — Tier B) for $15–20k |
| **Millions of users, ads only** | Out of band | Wrong scale for this card’s account constraint |
| **Pay-per-job cash** that *feels* monthly | **Yes if productised** | Convert to retainers / contracts / lead subscriptions so it stops being feast-famine |

*No new landscape claims here — only implications for $15–20k / 15–40 accounts.*

### 5.1 Need vs pay

| Signal | Implication for MRR |
|--------|---------------------|
| MBIE: only **18%** prioritise “become more digital”; **36%** feel profitability squeeze (costs + weaker customer acquisition); barriers = cost/time/uncertainty | Selling “digital” as virtue loses. Selling **customers / cost / time** can win. (Paid-pain §7; Step 1) |
| MBIE: ~**40%** use SEO/ads — gap is real | Gap ≠ WTP. Agency-lite and packs must prove paid requests (paid-pain filter 1) |
| IR 2024: **91%** of surveyed SMEs already pay **external** tax/compliance help | Compliance wallets exist — **already owned** by accountants/Hnry/Xero. Adjacent education OK; cloning IR fails |
| CPA NZ (Tier B): accountants = primary advice channel; felt tech ROI weak (**26%** say tech improved profitability) | Accountant-handed distribution > cold “AI tool” pitch for compliance-adjacent offers |
| Substitutes: Trade Me, Builderscrack, SEEK, Groups, Neighbourly, Xero, agencies, Canstar/Glimp-class | Any connect/lead model must name **who already gets paid** and why a thinner slice still clears |

### 5.2 Geography colour (not a suburb lock)

- Auckland region ~**1.82M** of ~**5.32M**; ~**37.5%** of GDP; ~**35%** of business locations/employees (Steps 0–1).
- Employment is **belted**, not only CBD: Penrose, East Tāmaki, North Harbour, Airport, Manukau, etc. (Census 2018 workplace colour — §6).
- Secondary cities (Christchurch, Hamilton, Wellington, Tauranga) can support **15 accounts** if ARPU is high — density ≠ lock.

### 5.3 Institutions / PSW as a score column only

- Always-on: FTA / CGA / Privacy / UEM (no harvested lists).
- Thick: FAP, REA, restricted trades, therapeutic claims.
- PSW category colour: open-work holders who **own** a business often **cannot employ others** — still **OPEN / verify** for this person (Step 2/5). Hiring-heavy rollups and labour-desk models stay high-friction until cleared.
- Formation is cheap; permission is not.

### 5.4 Apex / pSEO posture (this card)

pSEO + lead-gen architecture = **possible Later tool**, not mandate. Honesty-split rank-and-rent stays on the sheet; thin doorway farms fail the charter.

## 6. New web findings (only what you actually fetched this pass)

*Access date for this pass: **2026-09-14** unless noted. Labels mark tier.*

### 6.1 Agency / retainer pricing (Tier B vendor)

- NZ digital agencies commonly **$95–$200/hr**; retainers **$500–$1,500** (single channel), **$1,500–$4,000** (multi-channel), **$4,000–$10,000+** (full-service); ads management **$300–$1,500** flat or **10–20%** of spend. ([Jelly Digital blog, Sep 2026](https://www.jellydigital.nz/blog/digital-marketing-agency-cost-nz).)
- Published menu example: Google Ads / Meta from **$495/mo**; SEO from **$495–$1,995/mo**. ([Jelly Digital pricing](https://www.jellydigital.nz/pricing).)
- Virtual Marketers: retainers from **$2,400/mo + GST** (2 days/month). ([Virtual Marketers](https://www.virtualmarketers.co.nz/blog/budget-friendly-marketing-companies-startups-new-zealand).)
- Brand partnership retainers published **$1,499 / $2,799 / $4,599** /mo. ([Obvious Agency](https://obvious.agency/blog/what-a-brand-engagement-costs/).)

**Implication (INTENT):** $15–20k MRR ≈ **4–15** mid/full retainers **or** **15–30** single-channel seats — arithmetically inside NZ market norms. Delivery hours and PSW hiring remain the binding constraints.

### 6.2 Connect / lead fees (Tier B vendor)

- Builderscrack success fee (pre-subscription chase path): **7.5%** of first $2,500; **4.5%** of $2,500–$10,000; **1.5%** of $10,000–$50,000; min **$10 + GST**. ([Builderscrack Zendesk](https://builderscrack.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408147134095-How-is-a-success-fee-calculated).)
- Connection plans use **tokens** deducted when homeowner accepts connection; monthly plans exist (exact public plan $ on Help Centre is sparse; third-party writeups cite ~**$79–$149/mo** — treat as **UNKNOWN/verify** on Builderscrack itself). ([Builderscrack how-it-works/trade](https://builderscrack.co.nz/how-it-works/trade); [token policy](https://builderscrack.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/9823916559887-What-are-tokens-and-how-they-work).)

**Implication:** Consumer tradie leads are a **priced, incumbent market**. A solo desk competing on the same homeowner jobs fights Builderscrack + Groups. Commercial / B2B enquiry desks are a **different buyer**.

### 6.3 Commercial service contract pricing (Tier B vendor quotes)

- EcoSparks published guide (updated Nov 2025): small office weekly clean **$400–$700/mo**; medium **$600–$1,200**; large **$1,200–$3,000+**; hourly **$30–$40**. ([EcoSparks price list](https://www.ecosparks.co.nz/cleaning-price-list/).)
- Serviipro Auckland guide: similar bands; large/industrial **$1,200–$5,000+/mo**. ([Serviipro](https://serviipro.co.nz/cost-commerical-cleaning-auckland/).)
- Premium Clean Auckland: from **$35 + GST** /visit (small); medium **from $80 + GST** /visit. ([Premium Clean](https://www.premiumclean.co.nz/commercial-cleaning/auckland/).)

**Implication:** **25–40** small commercial contracts at **$400–$800/mo** gross can print **$15–20k+ revenue** — but that is **ops + labour**, not a digital product. Useful as **Later acquire** colour and as proof that **commercial ARPU** exists; not as a “don’t talk to people” SEO thesis.

### 6.4 Commercial prequal / tender friction (Tier A/B)

- SiteWise Level 3 (subcontractor prequal): **$250 + GST / year**; Level 2 **$795 + GST / year**. ([SiteWise pricing](https://sitewise.co.nz/pricing/).)
- Councils commonly require SiteWise ≥**75%** for on-site contractors (e.g. Whangārei DC). ([WDC Working with Council](https://www.wdc.govt.nz/Council/Working-with-Council).)
- TenderLink / GETS are the discovery rails for public/private tenders. ([TenderLink](https://illion.tenderlink.com/).)

**Implication:** SMEs already pay **time + small annual fees** to stay tenderable. A **capability / H&S / file pack** that reduces re-assembly per tender is a paid-pain-shaped product family — WTP beyond SiteWise = **UNKNOWN**.

### 6.6 Accountant-adjacent monthly packages (Tier B)

Published monthly accounting packages commonly **~$99–$565+/mo** (Xero + compliance ± advisory). Examples: Tax Hero from **$99**; First Class Accounts **$99–$499**; Wise Advice **$365–$565**; Advisory Accountants **$200–$500**. ([Tax Hero](https://www.taxhero.co.nz/pricing); [First Class Accounts](https://www.firstclassaccounts.co.nz/monthly-packages); [Wise Advice](https://www.wiseadvice.co.nz/business-accounting-package); [Advisory Accountants](https://www.advisoryaccountants.co.nz/plans-and-pricing.html).)

**Implication:** Compliance retainers already sit in the **$200–$500** band. Non-accountant operators should sell **adjacent artefacts** (A-P-4), not pretend to be a chartered practice (licence/FAP/PSW OPEN).

### 6.7 Media / membership / affiliate (Tier B)

- The Kākā (Bernard Hickey): regular **$19/mo** or **$190/year** (intro offers lower). ([The Kākā Substack](https://thekaka.substack.com/p/an-experiment-and-our-christmas-offer).)
- Consumer NZ: membership from about **$15/mo** framing on FAQs (advice line); exact current join prices change — verify on join page. ([Consumer NZ FAQs](https://www.consumer.org.nz/about-us/faqs).) Herald colour: membership base declined toward ~**38k** (older Media Insider piece — directional only).
- Idealog 2026 media kit: newsletter solus eDM **$3,000**; partnerships from **$12k/year**; display units **$700–$2,500**. ([Idealog media kit 2026 PDF](https://idealog.co.nz/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Idealog-media-kit-2026_web.pdf).)
- Canstar NZ monetises via **lead referrals**, **sponsored/promoted** placements, and **ratings/award licences** — not “free research charity.” ([Canstar — How we get paid](https://www.canstar.co.nz/how-we-get-paid/), updated 20 May 2026.)
- Glimp: free to users; revenue when users switch broadband. ([Glimp FAQ](http://www.glimp.co.nz/faq).)

**Implication:** Consumer membership at $15–19 is a **scale audience** game. Comparison/affiliate can fund $15–20k with **fewer commercial partners** if categories convert — but FAP-adjacent finance categories stay thick (Step 2). Prefer **non-FAP** verticals (tools, broadband-class, B2B software) when scoring.

### 6.8 Geography of commercial demand (Tier A + Auckland ED)

- Stats NZ (2018 Census workplace): Auckland CBD ~**99,420** workers; other large workplace SA2s include **Penrose ~24,675**, **East Tāmaki ~23,382**, **North Harbour ~23,163**, **Auckland Airport ~23,073**, **Manukau Central ~14,466**. ([Stats NZ — Christchurch CBD bouncing back](https://www.stats.govt.nz/news/newly-released-census-data-shows-christchurch-cbd-bouncing-back).)
- Auckland Economic Development place profiles: industrial growth stronger in **outer** precincts (Airport, Wiri, East Tāmaki/Highbrook, North Harbour); central industrial softer; office growth CBD-heavy. ([Auckland ED — Economic Place Profiles](https://aucklandeconomicdevelopment.com/economy-and-insights/research-and-reports/economic-place-profiles).)

**Implication for 15 accounts:** A **commercial-belt** sales radius (e.g. Penrose–East Tāmaki–Airport corridor) can contain enough premises for high-ARPU B2B without locking a suburb brand. Residential outskirts favour consumer leads / Groups competition.

### 6.9 Acquire / operate boring cash (Tier B broker colour)

- Cleaning businesses commonly marketed at **~$50k–$500k+** purchase; broker guides cite ~**2–3×** annual profit as a rough multiple. ([NZ BizBuySell guide](https://www.nzbizbuysell.co.nz/nz-business/buying/buying-a-cleaning-business).)
- Example listings (asking, not sold comps): residential cleaning ~**$129k** ask / ~**$93k** SDE; packing+cleaning ~**$299k** ask / ~**$160k+** profits / ~**$537k** revenue. ([Vendi listing](https://vendi.co.nz/property/11a-bayside-avenue-te-atatu-peninsula/); [Kakapo](https://kakapobusiness.co.nz/listing/premium-packing-cleaning-working-owner-profits-of-160-000).)
- Bizstats: support-services SDE multiples vary widely with **contract quality**. ([Bizstats admin & support](https://www.bizstats.co.nz/industries/administrative-and-support/).)

**Implication:** Path to $15–20k/mo **owner earnings** exists in boring services — **Later**, capital-heavy, hiring/PSW-critical. Score it; do not romanticise as the digital thesis.
