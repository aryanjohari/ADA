## 3. One-liner

A short filter so a solo NZ operator does **not** confuse useful pages, newsletters, or “we’ll do digital for you” with a business — and can design **one** capped experiment around a **repeating money/time loss** that could become a product later, without locking a niche or starting with an app.

---

## 4. Core research question

Given Steps 0–5, which **pain-shaped jobs** are even eligible for a first NZ experiment — such that strangers might **pay**, the job is **the same enough to automate later**, incumbents do not already own it as a default, and a solo operator can **finish the loop** without becoming the forever worker — and what experiment would **falsify** that pain in days/weeks (ads + real pages + a product-shaped offer), not months of unvalidated SEO?

## 7. Filter stack (apply in this order)

*Fail fast. Do not average into a score. Bands are **INTENT / HYPOTHESIS**, not market-winner EVIDENCE.*

| # | Filter | Pass | Fail |
|---|--------|------|------|
| **1. Paid pain** | Would they already pay **money, a wage, or lost sales** because this repeats? | “I lose jobs / I already pay a person or a tool for this mess” | “They *should* fix their Google / be more digital” (often $0 — YouTube/nephew/ignore) |
| **2. Repeatable** | Same job most weeks for many of the same *kind* of person? | 8-step listing; quote-then-chase; roster this week | Every client is a custom “fix my whole business” |
| **3. Substitutes** | Who already gets paid? (Step 3) | A thinner/simpler slice they **won’t** buy from Xero/Hnry/Tradify/SEEK/Builderscrack/accountant | Head-on vs those defaults |
| **4. Finish the loop** | Can *this* operator complete a yes **this month** without a fake marketplace? | Product-shaped pack **you** (or a verified contractor) can deliver; or intro **named** licensed people you already have | HVAC/plumber ads with nobody to send; “tool” promised but not built |
| **5. Automatable later** | Could a form / checklist / reminder / small tool do most of it after you learn the steps? | Yes in principle — **do not build it first** | Forever-bespoke consulting |
| **6. Asset, not you** | After ~10 paid loops, do hours-per-job **fall** while strangers still find the offer? | Pack / process / waitlist-for-tool | Calendar still *is* the product |
| **7. Institutions + PSW** | FTA/CGA/Privacy/UEM; thick licences; hiring | Baseline + match-to-licensed if needed | FAP/REA/restricted trades done by the operator; **employing others** until **OPEN/verify** clears |
| **8. Anti-doorway** | Real utility pages | 4–8 honest pages + who you are | One LP only **or** 50 city shells |

**INTENT:** Filter 1 before filter 5. “We can automate it” is useless if nobody pays. Filter 5 before you fall in love with a service.

### 7.1 Why “digital amateurishness” is not automatically a paid pain

- **EVIDENCE:** ~**40%** of NZ businesses promote with digital marketing / SEO / ads. Top support asks for digital enablement: **choosing appropriate/affordable tools** and **digital marketing**. ([MBIE — Digital capability of NZ businesses](https://www.mbie.govt.nz/business-and-employment/business/support-for-business/research-and-reports-for-small-business/digital-capability-of-new-zealand-businesses); [digital capability monitor PDF, Jul 2024](https://www.mbie.govt.nz/dmsdocument/28920-business-digital-capability-monitor-key-findings-and-update-july-2024-pdf). Accessed 2026-09-13.)
- **EVIDENCE:** Barriers that keep firms from being “as digitally enabled as they could be”: **information security/fraud** most frequent (**49%** in the 2023-framed monitor); **difficulty choosing tools** and **no time to learn** together cited by **50%**; **cost / value-for-money** concerns: **48%**. ([Same digital capability monitor PDF](https://www.mbie.govt.nz/dmsdocument/28920-business-digital-capability-monitor-key-findings-and-update-july-2024-pdf). Accessed 2026-09-13.)
- **EVIDENCE:** Only **18%** of surveyed businesses list “become more digital” as a priority (sixth-most in that 2024 motivations cut). **58%** report ongoing challenge (cashflow / growth slowdown); **36%** profitability squeeze (rising costs + weaker customer acquisition). Top support asked: tax breaks (**47%**), one-to-one mentoring (**32%**), online courses (**29%**). ([MBIE — Business health and motivations](https://www.mbie.govt.nz/business-and-employment/business/support-for-business/research-and-reports-for-small-business/business-health-and-motivations-research); [motivations PDF](https://www.mbie.govt.nz/dmsdocument/28986-business-health-and-digital-monitor-july-2024). Accessed 2026-09-13.)
- **HYPOTHESIS:** The digital gap is real **and** often not what they will pay for *this week*. Selling “we’ll do digital for you” fights stated priorities (costs, customers, time) and free substitutes. MBIE’s own framing: link digitalisation to **motivations they already have** (cost, growth, time) or adoption stays low.

## 8. Service vs product vs concierge (do not mix the labels)

| Shape | Homepage promise | What scale looks like | Role in *this* program |
|-------|------------------|----------------------|-------------------------|
| **Service** | “Hire me / we’ll do it for you” | More of **your** hours | Allowed as **samples** only (charter: cash ≠ asset thesis) |
| **Product** | “This repeating job gets faster/cheaper without hiring someone” | Same offer, falling hours per job | **Destination** |
| **Concierge MVP** | They think they’re buying **the system**; you still do it by hand for the first ~10 | Learns the steps for automation | **INTENT:** OK **behind the curtain**. Fail if the brand is “call Ada” |

**Startup-shaped sell (INTENT):** one sentence — *“NZ [who] still wastes [hours] / loses [money] every week on [this one task]. This makes that task smaller.”*  
Not: “We help SMEs with digital.”

## 9. Speed of proof (ads first; organic later)

**HYPOTHESIS:** A new domain with organic-only traffic for weeks often proves **nothing**. Step 5’s 6–8 week window is a **max**, not “wait for SEO.”

| Meter | Use for | Not for |
|-------|---------|---------|
| **Small Google Ads** (substantiated claims; FTA) | Days–2 weeks: does this **pain sentence + offer** get clicks and **requests**? | “Owning Google forever”; outspending Trade Me/SEEK wallets |
| **CTA / completed jobs** | Validation | Pageviews, “signed up for corrections” as the *product* |
| **Organic / GSC** | After the offer is real | First proof on a new site |

**EVIDENCE:** IAB NZ CY 2025 Search advertiser revenue **$1.44b** — rented attention exists (**Tier B**). Vertical CPC = **UNKNOWN** — **do not invent** a break-even in this card (Step 5). Ads spend sits on the **cash track** (charter); cap it so you cannot buy a fake yes.

**FTA (EVIDENCE):** Ads and pages are “in trade”; unsubstantiated claims and fake scarcity are prohibited (Step 2 / Consumer Protection — Fair Trading Act).

## 10. Experiment spec (one live loop; not implementation)

*This is a **design**. This file does not build the site, ads account, or ADA modules.*

### 10.1 Surface (trust, not one LP)

**INTENT:** One site. About + **4–8 useful receipt-quality pages** (sources, date, UNKNOWN, what you’d correct) + **one offer page**. Not a doorway farm. Trader identity clear if selling (ComCom selling-online colour — Step 5). Privacy statement if collecting; UEM consent if emailing (Step 4/5).

### 10.2 Offer (product-shaped)

One **named job**, fixed scope/price or waitlist — not “whatever you need.” Optional extra **buttons** (measure only):

| Button | Meaning | Fulfill only if… |
|--------|---------|------------------|
| **Do this job (pack)** | Product / concierge | You can finish it this week |
| **Intro a licensed person** | Match-side connect (A-C-2 / W-NOW-07) | You **already have** 1–3 named, licensed people who will take it |
| **Waitlist for the tool** | Later A-P-5 signal | Copy does **not** claim the app exists |

**INTENT:** Do not run HVAC/plumber ads without a real bench. Do not promise software.

### 10.3 Hard metrics (not newsletter vanity)

Count, in order of strength:

1. **Completed paid jobs** (or paid packs) — strongest  
2. **Qualified requests** (“do this pack”) — medium  
3. **Button mix** (pack vs intro vs waitlist) — directional  
4. **Ad click → request** cost (no invented “good” CPC)  
5. Signup / GSC — **secondary** (learning, not the product)

**Fail the experiment if:** clicks, no deliverable loop; or you deliver only as an open-ended consultant.

### 10.4 Cap (from Step 5, tightened)

| Rule | Bound (**INTENT**) |
|------|---------------------|
| Concurrent live offers | **1** pain / pack |
| Ads timebox for first read | **~2 weeks** then decide kill / continue |
| Max window before mandatory rethink | **6–8 weeks** (Step 5) |
| Sequential experiments before Step 5 refresh | **2** |
| Later software (W-LATER-01) | **0** until graduation (repeat job + WTP beyond a pack + substitutes don’t own it + PSW still OPEN/verify) |

### 10.5 Graduation (you → asset)

| After N paid loops (order of **10**, not a law) | Healthy | Unhealthy |
|------------------------------------------------|---------|-----------|
| Hours per completed job | Down (form, playbook, reminders) | Flat or up |
| How they find you | Ads + pages, not only mates | Only people you already know |
| What they buy | The **named pack / system** | “Can you just handle everything?” |

## 15. OPEN questions (≤10)

1. What is the operator’s **one** pain sentence that passes filters 1–2? (**OPERATOR** — not answered here)
2. For that sentence: who already gets paid (IR accountants / Xero / Tradify / SEEK / Groups — Step 3 + §7.2) and why a thinner slice still wins? (**HYPOTHESIS** until ads)
3. Ads: will a substantiated claim earn a **request** inside a tiny cap — without inventing CPC? (**UNKNOWN**)
4. After ~10 loops: did hours-per-job fall? (filter 6)
5. Button mix: pack vs intro vs tool waitlist — which is real? (**UNKNOWN** until live)
6. PSW / contracting / partners — **OPEN / verify**
7. Does “ask the Facebook group” beat the pack within 30 days? (Step 3 Q10)
8. Zero-click / AI answers vs useful receipts — **UNKNOWN** (G2)
9. Which G1–G11 **die** once one paid pack has 2 weeks of ad data?
10. After two sequential experiments, has anyone **asked for software** — vs operator boredom? (guards W-LATER-01)
