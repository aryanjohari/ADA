## Signal / organism purpose (reference know, cited)

Humans are signal-driven organisms. Food capture exists so ADA can later read **energy in vs targets**, **protein vs gym recovery**, and **honest partial micros** — not so the cortex can role-play a dietitian on every `meal_log`.

| Why log | What the signal is **for** |
|---------|----------------------------|
| Energy | Daily kcal vs FACTS targets — fuel for work, training, recovery. |
| Protein | Repair / satiety / gym synergy — later join to lift volume (P4), not invented at write. |
| Carbs / fat | Readily available fuel vs dense energy + fat-soluble vitamin carriers. |
| Key micros | Ca, Fe, Mg, K, D, B12, … as **gap-visible** research slots — null stays null (`honest_partial`). |
| Meal slot | Breakfast/lunch/dinner/snack habits over weeks — collection now, reflection later. |

**Interpretive classes only — no invented meal values.** A logged line’s kcal/protein/etc. come from FDC/OFF/cache scaled by grams. This card does not assign “eggs = N grams protein.” Phone METAL (`8d90b519…`) already showed 5 eggs → FDC `2707154` *Egg, whole, boiled or poached* → **31.0 g protein** on the receipt — that number lives in the log, not here.

**Personal informatics:** collection (this organ) is the daily path; integration/reflection (Dream digest, P4 charts) wait until logs are honest ([Li et al., CHI 2010](https://doi.org/10.1145/1753326.1753611); [Choe et al., CHI 2014](https://doi.org/10.1145/2556284.2556964)). Nutrient numbers on a line cite USDA FoodData Central ([FDC API](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/api-guide.html)) or Open Food Facts — not this prose.

| Claim | Tag | Pointer |
|-------|-----|---------|
| Collection vs reflection stages | **EVIDENCE** | [Li et al., 2010](https://doi.org/10.1145/1753326.1753611) |
| Self-trackers collect first, explore later | **EVIDENCE** | [Choe et al., 2014](https://doi.org/10.1145/2556284.2556964) |
