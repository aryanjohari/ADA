## Signal / organism purpose (reference know, cited)

Humans are signal-driven organisms. People capture exists so ADA can later read **who someone is** (alias-safe) and **what you told her about them** — a social graph, not a mind-read, not a CRM, not LinkedIn.

**Operator vision (encode, don’t fanfiction):** later = a real contact card (details, logged conversations, dues attach). **This organ first:** `person_id` + aliases + Confirm + notes. Not CRM / Clay / LinkedIn / OSINT.

People are the same **job** as food lines, gym sets, time blocks, habit ticks, and dues: one honest grain per named thing. Different **physics**: not grams, not kg×reps, not duration, not a `local_day` tick, not an open loop. A person is an **identity card** that persists across days. Dues glue is optional `people_ids` on an **existing** due; birthday **may mint one due**. Dues stay dues.
